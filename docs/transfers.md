# Transfers

Money moved between your own accounts shouldn't count as spending. The
**Transfers** screen detects these automatically: an outgoing charge on one
account matched by an equal, opposite credit on another account within a few
days (same currency), e.g. −2'000 CHF on Current ↔ +2'000 CHF on Savings.

- **Suggested Matches** lists candidate pairs — **Confirm** to link them.
- Confirmed transfers are excluded from spending totals and reports, and can be
  **unlinked** later.

Matched pairs share a transfer group id. Opening-balance entries are never
matched.
