# Accounts

Each account has a name, institution, currency, colour, and an optional import
profile. Manage them under **Manage → Accounts**; they also appear as children
of **Transactions** in the sidebar.

- **Starting balance** — optionally seed an account's balance. It's stored as a
  synthetic "Starting balance" transaction that counts toward the balance but is
  excluded from spending, reports, transfers, and reconciliation.
- **Work expense account** — mark one account as the place you post work
  expenses. Work-tagged card charges are reconciled against its line items (see
  [work-expenses.md](work-expenses.md)).

Balances are the sum of an account's non-rejected transactions and are shown on
the [dashboard](dashboard-settings-data.md).
