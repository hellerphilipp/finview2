# Categories & auto-tagging

Categories are user-defined with a name, colour, and SF Symbol, and support one
level of sub-categories (e.g. *Groceries › Supermarket*). A starter set is
seeded on first launch. Kinds are *spending*, *income*, or *transfer*.

**Learning auto-tagging**: when you categorize a transaction, Tally records the
merchant → category association (a merchant is inferred from the description,
typically the parenthetical part). Future transactions from the same merchant
are pre-**suggested** with a wand/`?` marker while pending. Accept with **⌘J**,
pick another category, or just **confirm** — confirming applies the suggestion.

The longest, most specific matching merchant pattern wins.
