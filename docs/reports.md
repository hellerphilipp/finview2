# Reports

The **Reports** screen shows spending by top-level category over time:

- A **stacked bar chart** — spending per category, per month.
- A **pivot table** — categories × months with row totals.

A **funnel menu** in the toolbar adjusts how work-tagged spend is treated
(the funnel fills in when a non-default filter is active):

- **Included** — counted in its normal category.
- **Excluded** — dropped from the report.
- **Separate** — grouped under its own "Work" series.

Transfers and opening balances are always excluded. Foreign-currency amounts are
reported in the account currency (the deducted amount).
